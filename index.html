Power returns for most after the Thanksgiving storm, with another storm on the way

Power outages from the Thanksgiving storm fell from a peak of more than 900,000 customers to just under 300,000 by Friday night, CBS reports, citing PowerOutage.us. PBS reports that most remaining outages were in Pennsylvania and Missouri, where high winds and snow toppled trees. Another East Coast storm is expected by Sunday evening, with up to 2 inches of rain and gusts near 65 mph, CBS reports.

Context

Hackers breached Cloud One, the Air Force’s main cloud computing platform, Reuters reported, citing unnamed sources, CBS and Gray News report. A U.S. official confirmed the breach, PBS reports. The Air Force said it does not comment on the security of specific networks and systems.

Still unknown: Who did it and what, if anything, was taken.

Gray News says Cloud One reportedly supports programs that handle personnel information, logistics and maintenance. U.S. disables another ship trying to get past its blockade of Iran

A U.S. military aircraft fired on the Palau-flagged ship Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman on Thursday, knocking out its propulsion, U.S. Central Command said, AP and Fox report. The command said the ship had left Iran’s port of Bandar Abbas and ignored several warnings, and that the crew was not hurt. It says it has redirected 158 ships since the blockade resumed in July.

PBS (AP)Fox

Context

A blockade uses military force to stop ships from entering or leaving ports. The U.S. began blockading Iranian ports in April, lifted it during talks with Iran in the summer and restarted it in July, AP reports. The war with Iran began at the end of February and has been in a quiet stalemate for months, AP says. Iran’s navy commander said Iran will act to break the blockade.

Mexico complains about a U.S. tip line for reporting cartels

Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum said Friday that her government filed a formal complaint with the U.S. over a new Drug Enforcement Administration tip line, AP and CBS report. It lets people anonymously report cartel members, corrupt officials and money launderers in Sinaloa state, CBS reports. Sheinbaum called it interference and said the relationship is one of “coordination, not subordination.”

PBS (AP)CBS (AFP)Fox

Context

The DEA is the U.S. agency that fights illegal drugs. A cartel is a criminal group, often in the drug trade. Sinaloa is a Mexican state. DEA chief Terry Cole announced the tip line on X, CBS and AP report. He said a similar line for Jalisco state would follow, CBS and Fox report. The head of a Sinaloa journalists’ group welcomed it, AP reports.

FCC approves SpaceX plan for up to 1 million data-center satellites

The Federal Communications Commission on Wednesday approved SpaceX’s request to launch up to 1 million satellites meant to work as data centers in space, AP and CBS report. FCC Chairman Brendan Carr announced the approval on X. SpaceX must launch at least one by 2033 and add more through 2040, AP reports. Astrophysicist Adam Becker called the plan “a fantasy.”

PBS (AP)CBS

Context

A data center is a building full of computers that store and process information, and AI programs need huge amounts of that computing. SpaceX is Elon Musk’s rocket company. The FCC calls the satellites “orbital compute modules.” The American Astronomical Society warned they would be visible in the night sky, and Viasat and Amazon’s Kuiper Systems asked the FCC to turn down the request, AP reports.

Science & Health

EPA finds no lead in water samples after the Army’s Iowa detonation

The Environmental Protection Agency found no lead in 38 water samples from public water supplies taken after the Army destroyed a stockpile of lead azide in Iowa on Wednesday, CBS and Fox report. EPA chief Lee Zeldin called earlier air readings, which found no airborne lead, “a best-case scenario.” CBS says tests for other compounds and some home samples are still to come.

CBSFox

Context

Lead azide is a chemical used to set off ammunition since World War I. The EPA said this supply had become easier to set off by accident after about 20 years in storage. Lead can harm health, especially children’s. The Army set off about 5 tons at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown. Residents and environmental groups had tried to stop it in court, CBS reports.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
