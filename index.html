Politics

House sets aside the effort to impeach Hegseth

The House voted 211-204 on Tuesday to send Rep. Thomas Massie’s impeachment resolution against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the Judiciary Committee, which effectively ends it, CBS and Fox report. Massie was the only Republican to vote against that move, both outlets report. Hegseth called the effort a “political spectacle,” and Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries said it was “just the beginning,” CBS reports.

Republicans pick Jim Jordan to lead in the House and keep John Thune in the Senate

House Republicans chose Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio as their leader over Rep. Steve Scalise on Tuesday, 106 to 73 according to people familiar with the count, CBS and Fox report. Senate Republicans reelected John Thune as their leader after a push led by Sen. Ron Johnson to delay the vote fell short, AP and Fox report.

U.S. News

U.S. charges three men in an Iran-linked plot against a British air base

Federal prosecutors unsealed charges Tuesday accusing three men of plotting attacks for an Iran-aligned network, including a September plan against RAF Fairford, a British air base used by the U.S. Air Force, AP and Fox report. Prosecutors say Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood al-Saadi helped coordinate it from a New York jail. He is the only one of the three in custody, AP reports.

PBS (AP)Fox

Context

Prosecutors also accuse the men of helping organize attacks on Jewish communities in Europe earlier this year, both outlets report. The case names Iran’s Revolutionary Guard Corps and Kata’ib Hizballah, an Iraqi armed group. Al-Saadi pleaded not guilty in June to earlier charges, AP reports. Iran denies involvement. Charges are accusations, not proof of guilt.

Kansas

Holscher starts her transition while the governor’s race stays uncalled

Democrat Cindy Holscher has begun her transition to the governor’s office, including a meeting with Gov. Laura Kelly, KWCH and KAKE report, though the race has not been called. Holscher leads Republican Ty Masterson by about 7,600 votes in unofficial results, KWCH reports, and KAKE puts the gap under 8,000. Masterson has not conceded, both outlets report.

Context

Kansas requires an extra audit, a hand check of sample ballots, when the top two candidates are within 1% of all votes cast, both outlets report. The state board of canvassers finalizes results Nov. 16, and candidates can ask for a recount until 5 p.m. Nov. 17, KWCH reports. Holscher’s campaign has started calling her “Governor-elect,” KWCH reports. Masterson is president of the Kansas Senate.

World News

Russia and Ukraine hold U.S.-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi

Russian and Ukrainian delegations met with U.S. envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff in Abu Dhabi on Tuesday, with a second day set for Wednesday, AP and Fox report. They are the first three-way talks among the countries in nine months, both outlets report. Tuesday’s session lasted about four hours, AP reports.

PBS (AP)Fox

Context

Earlier rounds this year produced no major breakthroughs, AP reports.

U.S. soldier arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying linked to Russia

British police arrested a 41-year-old U.S. Army soldier near Gloucester, England, on Tuesday on suspicion of offenses linked to Russia, AP and CBS report. He is suspected of helping a foreign intelligence service and obtaining or disclosing protected information under a 2023 British law. He has not been named. FBI Director Kash Patel called it an “important arrest,” AP reports.

PBS (AP)CBS

Context

Espionage means spying: secretly gathering or passing on information for another government. A U.S. official told CBS he is a senior officer based at Imjin Barracks, home to a NATO headquarters. Police say the case is not linked to the Iran-tied plot at RAF Fairford, also near Gloucester, AP reports. A Kremlin spokesman said Russia had “nothing to do with” the arrest, CBS reports.

Business

Prices rose a bit faster than expected in October

Consumer prices rose 0.4% from September to October, more than the 0.3% economists expected, the Labor Department reported Tuesday, AP and CBS report. Core prices, which leave out food and energy, rose 0.3%. No yearly rate was published, because the 2025 government shutdown left no October 2025 data to compare against, both outlets report.

PBS (AP)CBS

Context

Inflation is the pace at which prices rise. The Federal Reserve can raise interest rates to slow it, which makes borrowing cost more. The Fed raised rates in September, AP reports, and meets again Dec. 8-9. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said the Fed should consider a half-point increase, both outlets report. Gas prices have fallen this month, AP and CBS report.

Science & Health

Scientists say the Iran war’s oil price jump cut carbon pollution slightly

Carbon dioxide from burning fossil fuels fell 0.4% this year, Global Carbon Project scientists estimate, AP reported Tuesday. They link the drop mainly to high oil prices from the Iran war. Oil emissions fell nearly 2% while coal rose 0.6%, and India’s emissions rose 4.4%. Counting deforestation and fires, total emissions still rose 1%, AP reports.

One source: Only AP’s report (on PBS) was found. No CBS, Fox or Axios story turned up, and NPR wouldn’t load.

PBS (AP)

Context

Carbon dioxide from burning coal, oil and gas traps heat and warms the planet. The Global Carbon Project’s yearly estimate draws on more than 140 scientists, AP reports. It is only the third drop this century, after the 2009 recession and the 2020 pandemic. Glen Peters, a scientist at Norway’s CICERO climate research center, called it good news “for the wrong reasons.”

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
