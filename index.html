Politics

Speaker Johnson won’t run to lead House Republicans

House Speaker Mike Johnson will not seek to lead Republicans in the new Congress, CBS and AP reported Wednesday, after Democrats won the House. CBS says he told staff on a call Wednesday morning. Majority Leader Steve Scalise and Judiciary Chairman Jim Jordan both told colleagues in letters they want the job. A GOP aide told CBS the vote is planned for Tuesday, Nov. 10.

CBSPBS (AP)

Context

When a party loses the House, its top member becomes minority leader instead of speaker. Johnson, from Louisiana, became speaker in 2023 after colleagues removed Kevin McCarthy, AP reports. He is expected to stay in the House as a regular member. In October he told CBS he meant to stay on as leader if Republicans lost.

Mississippi Senate race still too close to call

Democrat Scott Colom leads Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith, but neither CBS nor Fox has called the race. CBS reported Wednesday night that Colom led by just over 1,100 votes with about 96% counted. Fox reported Wednesday afternoon that about 44,000 ballots were still outstanding, and absentee ballots postmarked by Election Day can arrive through Nov. 10.

Reports differ: The size of the lead, which changed during the day. In a Wednesday afternoon report, Fox gave Colom a 43-vote lead; CBS’s later report says just over 1,100.

Context

Hyde-Smith, 67, has served since 2018. Colom is a district attorney. Mississippi has not elected a Democrat to the Senate since 1982, and Trump won the state by 20 points in 2024, CBS reports. Fox reports Democratic lawyer Marc Elias is leading a legal effort for Colom, and Mississippi Democrats filed a court challenge over long lines and ballot shortages in DeSoto County.

Kansas

Kansas governor’s race too close to call; audit ordered

Democrat Cindy Holscher led Republican Ty Masterson by 546,587 votes to 541,030 Wednesday, state results show, and the race had not been called. Holscher claimed victory. Masterson has not conceded and said the result needs “another look.” Because the margin is within 1%, Kansas law requires a close-race audit, KWCH and Kansas Reflector report.

KCUR (Kansas Reflector)WIBWKWCH

Context

In a close-race audit, counties recheck ballots from 10% of their precincts. Counties must certify results 6 to 13 days after the election, and Masterson could then ask for a full recount; the state pays if the margin is within half a percent. Masterson is president of the Kansas Senate, and Holscher is a state senator from Overland Park.

Democrats break the Republican supermajority in the Kansas House

Democrats gained about 20 seats in the Kansas House, leaving Republicans ahead 68 to 57 in unofficial results, KWCH, Kansas Reflector and the Johnson County Post report. Republicans had held 88 seats to 37. Democrat Megan Phillips leads House Majority Leader Chris Croft, 51% to 49%. Three House races face close-race audits.

KWCHKAKE (Kansas Reflector)KCUR (Johnson County Post)

Context

A supermajority here means two-thirds of the 125 seats. With it, Republicans could override Democratic Gov. Laura Kelly’s vetoes and put changes to the state constitution on the ballot without Democratic votes. Democratic leader Brandon Woodard says Democrats can now block such amendments and get more committee seats.

Israel’s president starts talks on a new government

Israeli President Isaac Herzog began meeting party leaders Wednesday, AP and Fox report, after accepting the certified results of the Oct. 27 election, AP reports. Final results give Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s bloc 53 of 120 seats and Gadi Eisenkot’s opposition 48, Fox reports, with 61 needed to govern. Arab-led parties won 19 seats.

Science & Health

NASA’s Swift telescope falls back to Earth

NASA’s Swift space telescope reentered the atmosphere over Indonesia on Wednesday, CBS and AP report. CBS says it happened at 7:39 a.m. Eastern over North Sumatra. Much of the roughly 1.5-ton spacecraft burned up, CBS reports; some denser parts may have survived. Neither outlet reports any damage. A robot rescue mission failed earlier this year.

CBSPBS (AP)

Context

Swift launched in 2004 to study gamma-ray bursts, huge flashes of energy from exploding stars. It was planned to last two years and lasted more than 20, AP reports. Solar activity pulled its orbit lower, AP reports. It also alerted the Hubble and Webb telescopes to events worth a closer look. Hubble is losing altitude too, AP reports.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
