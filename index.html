Science & Health

U.S. measles cases top 5,000 this year

The CDC counted 5,182 confirmed measles cases in the U.S. in 2026 as of Nov. 5, its tracker shows. That is the most in more than three decades, CBS reports, and more than double 2025’s 2,289 cases. The CDC lists 43 outbreaks this year, and about 96% of cases are linked to outbreaks. CBS reports most cases are in unvaccinated people.

CDCCBS

Context

Measles is a very contagious virus that causes fever and a rash, and the MMR vaccine prevents it. The U.S. declared measles eliminated in 2000, meaning it no longer spread year-round here, and CBS reports ongoing outbreaks put that status at risk. CBS reports about 92.4% of kindergartners got the MMR vaccine last school year, down from 95.2% in 2019-2020.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
