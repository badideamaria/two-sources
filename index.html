Politics

Van Hollen challenges Schumer to lead Senate Democrats

Sen. Chris Van Hollen of Maryland announced Thursday that he will challenge Sen. Chuck Schumer to lead Senate Democrats, CBS and Fox report. Van Hollen said Democrats need “new leadership” and criticized Schumer for getting involved in party primaries. Schumer wrote on X, “I have the votes to continue serving as Democratic Leader,” both outlets report. Sen. Bernie Sanders endorsed Van Hollen, CBS reports.

CBSFox

Context

Each party in the Senate picks its own leader. With Democrats holding the majority in January, their leader becomes majority leader and sets much of the Senate’s schedule. The leadership election is expected in early December, CBS reports. Van Hollen also wants Democrats to use the Dec. 11 government funding deadline to press the Trump administration over the Iran war, CBS reports. Sen. Brian Schatz backs Schumer, Fox reports.

Brooklyn building collapse toll rises to eight as rescue work ends

The death toll from Wednesday’s building collapse in Brooklyn, New York, rose to eight, including two children, AP and CBS New York report. Mayor Zohran Mamdani said 18 people were injured, including four firefighters. The fire department said it finished rescue operations Thursday afternoon after searching the whole site and finding no more victims, CBS reports. The cause is still unknown.

Context

Yesterday’s edition reported at least six dead. The three-story building fell while workers were digging on the lot next door, AP reports. The city’s Department of Buildings is investigating, and the Brooklyn district attorney’s office is looking at whether any crime contributed, CBS reports. City housing records list 98 open violations, or unfixed code problems, at the building that fell, CBS reports.

World News

Iranian clerics urge a look at leaving the nuclear treaty as Trump warns of strikes

Iran’s Assembly of Experts urged the country’s leaders on Thursday to consider leaving the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty, CBS and Fox report. It warned any new attack would get a response “without geographical limitations,” Fox reports. President Trump told Salem News Channel he would renew strikes with “no hesitation” if Iran threatens U.S. allies or shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, Fox reports.

CBS (live updates)Fox (live updates)

Context

The Non-Proliferation Treaty, or NPT, is a worldwide agreement meant to stop the spread of nuclear weapons. Countries without them promise not to build them. The Assembly of Experts is a council of clerics that chooses and oversees Iran’s supreme leader, who has the final say. The Strait of Hormuz is a narrow sea route for oil. On Truth Social, Trump wrote, “We’ve got their money,” CBS reports.

Jensen Huang resigned Thursday as Nvidia’s chief executive, AP and CBS report. CBS says it took effect immediately. Chief financial officer Colette Kress will serve as interim CEO, and Harvey Jones was named the company’s first board chair. Nvidia shares fell about 14% at the opening bell, then recovered some and closed down about 4%, AP reports. CBS puts the close at down 4.3%.

PBS (AP)CBS

Context

An interim CEO serves until a permanent one is chosen, and the board will consider inside and outside candidates.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
