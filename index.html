Democrat Scott Colom wins Mississippi’s Senate race

Democrat Scott Colom defeated Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith in Mississippi, according to calls by AP, Fox News and CBS News more than a week after Election Day. In a video, Hyde-Smith said she respects the decision of Mississippi voters and congratulated Colom, CBS and Fox report. Colom will be Mississippi’s first Black U.S. senator since Reconstruction, AP and CBS report.

Building collapse in Brooklyn kills at least six

A three-story building on Flatbush Avenue in Brooklyn, New York, collapsed just after 4 p.m. Wednesday, killing at least six people and injuring 19, including a firefighter, CBS New York and Fox report. Four of the injured were in critical condition. Officials said it is not known how many people may still be trapped. The cause is under investigation, both outlets report.

CBS New YorkFox

Context

More than 250 firefighters and medical workers responded, both report, and officials told CBS the search could last days.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
