Politics

Democrat Victoria Broderick wins Tennessee’s 4th District, unseating Rep. Scott DesJarlais

AP called Tennessee’s 4th District for Democrat Victoria Broderick on Tuesday, Fox’s results page shows, and CBS reports that Republican Rep. Scott DesJarlais lost to her. Democrats have now flipped 48 House seats and Republicans 3, CBS reports. Fox’s results page shows Democrats with 259 seats and Republicans with 173.

Fox (AP calls)CBS

Context

To “flip” a seat means a different party wins it than held it before. The 4th District, in Middle and East Tennessee, was redrawn before this election and became somewhat less Republican, CBS reports. President Trump carried the district by 24 points, CBS reports, citing the Cook Political Report. CBS says winners in a handful of House races have not been projected yet.

U.S. News

Fort Hood shooter Nidal Hasan asks a federal court to delay his Dec. 3 execution

Nidal Hasan, convicted in the 2009 Fort Hood shooting, asked a federal court on Tuesday to delay his execution by Army firing squad, set for Dec. 3, CBS and Fox report. He wants time to challenge his death sentence. The case went to Judge Tony Mattivi in Kansas, where Hasan is held at Fort Leavenworth, and a hearing was set for Wednesday morning, CBS reports.

CBS (with AP)Fox

Context

Hasan was convicted in 2013. Fox reports the ACLU is acting as standby counsel, meaning lawyers ready to step in. Hasan claims President Trump and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth improperly influenced his case, CBS reports. It would be the first U.S. military execution since 1961, CBS reports. He wants time to file a habeas corpus petition, Fox reports, which asks a court to decide whether someone is held lawfully.

A strong coastal storm is forecast for the East Coast on Thanksgiving

Heavy rain and high winds were expected to begin Wednesday night along the Carolinas coast, reach the New York City area on Thanksgiving Day and Maine by Friday, National Weather Service meteorologist Joe Wegman told AP in a report published Monday. CBS Philadelphia forecasts wind gusts of 50 to 60 mph around Philadelphia and near 70 mph at the Jersey Shore, with coastal flooding possible.

PBS (AP)CBS Philadelphia

Context

Coastal flooding happens when wind pushes ocean water onto land, often worst at high tide. On the West Coast, very high “king tides” could bring flooding from Washington state to Southern California, AP reports. A high wind watch means damaging winds are possible; a warning means they are expected. The middle of the country is expected to stay dry, AP reports.

A Russian surveillance drone briefly crossed into Poland from Ukraine on Tuesday before turning back, CBS and Fox report. Deputy Defense Minister Paweł Zalewski said Poland assumes it was intentional and chose not to shoot it down to avoid risk to people on the ground. It came days after Russian missiles fell on Polish soil, both report.

Polish prosecutors said missile debris came from Russian Kh-101 cruise missiles, Fox reports.

The Vatican publishes Pope Francis’s private diary

“The Private Diary of Pope Francis” was released Tuesday, AP and Fox report. It covers 1992 to August 2024 and describes a spiritual crisis in 2021 and 2022 tied to the temptation of power, and times Francis believed he encountered the devil. Cardinal Víctor Manuel Fernández compiled it. Passages Francis wrote in code are left out, AP reports.

PBS (AP)Fox

Context

Pope Francis led the Catholic Church from 2013 until he died in April 2025. Pope Leo XIV, who followed Francis, approved its release, AP reports. Francis wrote of seeing “the entrance to hell” during a 2018 abuse scandal in Chile’s church, AP reports.

Business

Jury convicts Huawei of conspiring to steal trade secrets and of bank fraud

A federal jury convicted Chinese tech company Huawei on Tuesday of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, AP and Fox report. Jurors acquitted it of racketeering conspiracy, the most serious charge. Fox, citing Reuters, reports the Brooklyn jury convicted Huawei on 10 of 12 counts. Huawei lawyer Brian Heberlig said the company will appeal and called the acquittal a vindication, AP reports.

PBS (AP)Fox (Reuters, live updates)

Context

A trade secret is private business information, like product designs, that gives a company an edge. Racketeering laws are usually used against organized crime. Prosecutors said Huawei copied a competitor’s code and misled banks about business in Iran, AP reports. Huawei’s lawyers said a few employees’ wrongdoing was turned into a false story of a conspiracy. Huawei can’t sell equipment to U.S. carriers over security concerns.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
