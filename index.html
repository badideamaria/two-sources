Politics

Votes are still being counted. This edition covers calls by CBS, AP and Fox as of early Wednesday. A projection is an outlet’s call from the votes counted so far; official results come later.

CBSPBS (AP)

Democrats win control of the Senate

CBS News projected late Tuesday that Democrats won control of the Senate, and AP and Fox News also report Democrats took it. Fox reports Democrats flipped Republican-held seats in Texas, North Carolina, South Carolina, Ohio, Kansas, Iowa and Maine; AP lists the same six besides Maine.

Still unknown: How large the majority will be. Races in Alaska and Mississippi had not been called early Wednesday, CBS and Fox report.

Context

The Senate has 100 seats. Before the election Republicans held 53 and Democrats 47, CBS reports, so Democrats needed a net gain of four. A net gain counts seats won minus seats lost. The Senate votes on the president’s choices for judges and Cabinet jobs, and a bill must pass both the Senate and the House to become law.

Collins loses in Maine; El-Sayed wins in Michigan

CBS and AP report Democrat Troy Jackson defeated Republican Sen. Susan Collins of Maine. AP reports she conceded after calling him. Collins said serving Maine “has been the honor of my life,” AP reports. In Michigan, CBS projects Democrat Abdul El-Sayed beat Republican Mike Rogers, and Fox also reports him as the projected winner.

CBSCBS live blogPBS (AP)Fox

Context

Collins, 73, was first elected in 1996 and was seeking a sixth term, AP reports. She was the only Republican in Congress from New England. Jackson, 58, is a fifth-generation logger and a former president of the Maine state Senate. He became the nominee in July after Graham Platner withdrew. El-Sayed is a doctor; Fox reports he will be the first Muslim U.S. senator.

AP also calls the House for Democrats

New since yesterday’s edition: AP reported early Wednesday that Democrats won the House, joining CBS News, which projected it, and Fox News, which also reports Democrats won the House. CBS made its projection at 10:15 p.m. Eastern Tuesday. About 75 House races were still undecided at 4:19 a.m., CBS reports, so the size of the majority isn’t known.

Context

The House has 435 seats, and a party needs 218 for a majority. AP says it calls House control only when one party reaches 218 seats. Before the vote, Democrats needed a net gain of three seats, AP reported. Republican Rep. Tom Kean of New Jersey was among those who lost, AP reports.

Trump congratulates Democrats and talks of vetoes

President Trump told CBS News, “congratulations to the Democrats,” and said he looks forward to working with them, or else he would “veto everything.” On Fox News he said Democrats “did a good job” and blamed some Republican candidates, AP reports. He posted that Republicans would have won if he had been on the ballot. CBS reports he told Fox “part of it was Iran.”

Context

A veto is the president’s power to reject a bill Congress has passed. Congress can still make it law with a two-thirds vote in both the House and Senate. Early exit polls, which survey voters after they vote, put Trump’s approval at 38%, CBS reports. AP reports Trump called Texas Gov. Greg Abbott a “RINO,” meaning Republican in name only, after Abbott lost.

U.S. News

Escaped Tennessee prisoner caught in Missouri

Jacob Morley, 28, who escaped a Tennessee prison on Oct. 25, was arrested Tuesday in Missouri, CBS and Fox report. CBS says the arrest was near Norborne, about an hour from Kansas City. Fox reports officers used road cameras and spotted his truck in Liberty, and he got stuck trying to veer off the road. He was serving a life sentence for murder.

CBSFox

Context

Morley slipped through a fence at the Northwest Correctional Complex in Tiptonville, Tennessee, CBS reports. He was convicted of first-degree murder in the 2022 killing of a former co-worker. The Missouri State Highway Patrol made the arrest with other agencies. The Tennessee Bureau of Investigation, a state police agency, announced it, CBS reports.

Floods damage towns in the Four Corners region

Almost two weeks of on-and-off heavy rain flooded parts of the Four Corners region, AP reports. In Pagosa Springs, Colorado, a home was swept off its foundation and bridges collapsed or were damaged. CBS reports a Colorado rescue team was sent Monday after floods washed away homes. AP reports one person died in floodwaters.

PBS (AP)CBS Colorado

Context

The Four Corners is where Arizona, Colorado, New Mexico and Utah meet. AP reports the rain fell on ground already soaked from an especially wet fall. In San Juan County, New Mexico, evacuation orders covered at least 20,000 people and were likely to be lifted soon, AP reports. Cleanup in Pagosa Springs could take weeks or more.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
