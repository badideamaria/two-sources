Politics

Polls have closed in most states and votes are still being counted. This edition covers results outlets had projected by about 11 p.m. Eastern. A projection is a news outlet’s call from the votes counted so far. Official results come later.

CBSFox

Democrats projected to win the House

CBS News and the Fox News Decision Desk both project that Democrats will control the House in the next Congress. CBS says Democrats needed a net gain of three seats. Fox’s tally showed seven seats flipped to Democrats so far. AP had not called House control when I checked; PBS reports AP waits until a party reaches 218 seats.

Still unknown: How many seats each party will end up with. Many races were still being counted, and AP reports New York’s 17th District count is delayed until Wednesday by ballot-scanner problems.

CBSFoxPBS (AP)

Context

The House has 435 seats, and 218 is a majority. Republicans hold it now. The majority party chooses the speaker, who decides which bills get a vote, so control matters for what Congress passes. To “project” a winner, an outlet studies the votes counted so far and judges that the trailing side can’t catch up. Different outlets make these calls at different times.

Democrats flip Senate seats; control not yet decided

CBS and Fox project Democrats won Republican-held seats in North Carolina, where Roy Cooper beat Michael Whatley, and South Carolina, where Annie Andrews beat Sen. Darline Graham. In Texas, Democrat James Talarico led Ken Paxton about 60% to 39% with two-thirds counted, Fox reports; CBS projects Talarico wins, but Fox hasn’t called it. Democrats held Georgia and New Hampshire; Republicans held Florida and Kentucky.

Still unknown: Which party will control the Senate, and the final result in Texas. Maine, Michigan, Iowa, Ohio and Alaska were also not called.

CBSFox

Context

The Senate has 100 seats, two per state, and about a third are up for election every two years. Republicans hold 53 now. If the Senate splits 50-50, the vice president, Republican JD Vance, breaks ties, so Democrats need 51 seats for control. To “flip” a seat means winning one the other party held. CBS says Democrats must gain four seats overall.

Democrats flip House seats in several states

In Pennsylvania, Democrats Bob Brooks, Paige Cognetti and Janelle Stelson defeated Republican Reps. Ryan Mackenzie, Rob Bresnahan Jr. and Scott Perry. Democrat Rebecca Bennett beat Rep. Tom Kean Jr. in New Jersey. Democrat Zach Dembo won Kentucky’s 6th District, an open seat Republicans held.

CBSPBS (AP)PBS (AP)Fox

Context

Each House district elects one representative for a two-year term. An “open seat” is one where the current member didn’t run again. Republicans have held the House since the 2023 Congress, with a small majority, so a handful of flips can change who is in charge. Scott Perry once led the House Freedom Caucus, a group of conservative Republicans.

Ohio, Georgia and Florida choose governors

Democrat Amy Acton defeated Republican Vivek Ramaswamy for Ohio governor, CBS and Fox project. In Georgia, Democrat Keisha Lance Bottoms beat Republican Rick Jackson, CBS projects and AP reports; AP says she is the first Black woman elected governor of any state. In Florida, Republican Byron Donalds beat Democrat David Jolly, CBS, Fox and AP report.

CBSFoxPBS (AP)

Context

Governors run state governments, much as the president runs the federal one. Amy Acton is a doctor who led Ohio’s health department early in the COVID-19 pandemic. Vivek Ramaswamy is a businessman who ran for president in 2024. Keisha Lance Bottoms is a former mayor of Atlanta. Byron Donalds is a congressman from Florida, and Fox describes him as Trump-backed.

U.S. News

Man charged over powder letters to officials

Robin Martin of Oklahoma was charged with conveying a hoax and mailing threatening communications, AP and CBS report. Investigators link him to more than 700 envelopes of powder sent to election officials, members of Congress and others. The FBI says the powder was harmless, such as yeast or flour, though AP notes not every letter has been fully tested. He was arrested Monday.

PBS (AP)CBSFox

Context

A hoax charge can apply when someone sends something made to look dangerous, like fake poison, to frighten people. AP reports the letters warned of treason and claimed Donald Trump won the 2020 election, which Joe Biden won. AP says election offices in Louisiana and Oklahoma were evacuated after receiving them. No lawyer for Martin was identified, AP reports.

Florida jet’s tail came apart before it crashed

Most of the crashed Learjet’s left elevator, a tail part, was found 1.3 to 3.8 miles from the impact site in Palm Beach County, NTSB member Tom Chapman said Tuesday. It was in three pieces. AP reports Chapman said the find confirms the jet was breaking apart before it hit the ground. Asked about nearby Mar-a-Lago, he said there was no evidence of a connection.

Still unknown: The cause. AP reports a preliminary report is expected in about a month, and finding the cause will likely take more than a year.

PBS (AP)Fox

Context

The elevator is a movable flap on a plane’s tail that tips the nose up or down. Fox reports Chapman doubted the jet could have recovered even with it attached, given how fast it was falling. The NTSB, or National Transportation Safety Board, investigates crashes. The jet was flying from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas when it crashed Sunday, killing all nine aboard. Mar-a-Lago is President Trump’s Florida home.

World News

Trump says U.S. aid to Israel will end

President Trump told Fox News on Tuesday that the U.S. will stop giving Israel $4 billion a year but will keep selling it weapons. He said “it gets cut off at the end of the year,” and AP reports the change would take effect in 2027. Trump said Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu raised the idea. Netanyahu’s office had no immediate comment, AP reports.

Still unknown: How the current aid agreement would change. Fox reports the timing and terms have not been detailed.

PBS (AP)Fox

Context

The U.S. has given Israel military aid for decades. Fox reports the current agreement provides about $3.8 billion a year through 2028, and Congress still has to approve the money each year. Trump said aid to Israel had become a Democratic talking point and named Abdul El-Sayed, the Democratic Senate candidate in Michigan.

Miami tops first College Football Playoff rankings

The selection committee’s first rankings put Miami (8-0) first, followed by Georgia, Utah, Indiana and Alabama. Ohio State is 12th but would miss the 12-team field if the season ended now, because Boise State, ranked 25th, would take one of the places guaranteed to conference champions.

Context

A committee ranks the top college football teams each week, and its final ranking sets the 12-team playoff bracket. The five highest-ranked conference champions are guaranteed spots, so a lower-ranked champion can push out a higher-ranked team. Indiana is the reigning national champion. Fox notes Miami plays Notre Dame, ranked 11th, on Saturday.

How this is made

  • Claude, an AI, opens the articles and writes each story in its own words. It can make mistakes, so every story links to its sources.
  • A fact goes in only if two separate outlets report it. If only one does, the story says so. Two outlets running the same wire story count as one.
  • Before an edition goes up, a separate Claude session that didn’t write it rechecks every story against the articles. No person reads it first, so mistakes can still get through.
  • Context notes explain the background in plainer words. Claude writes them from the articles and long-settled facts, and they don’t have their own source links.
  • Claims and opinions get a name on them. Describing words like “stunning” are not allowed.
  • Choosing which stories to include is still a judgment call. That part can’t be fully objective.
  • AP, Reuters, BBC, The Guardian and The Wall Street Journal block Claude’s search tool, so they are only used where another outlet carries their stories.
